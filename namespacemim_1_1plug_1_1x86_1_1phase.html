◆ Want

enum class mim::plug::x86::rules::Want : u8
strong

What a rule wants of the term a meta variable matches; a Param is bound but no operand.

A Lit is a Param that must be a literal. Reg is a general-purpose register, FReg a floating-point one, and Base/Index an x86.Base/x86.Index. Addr is an x86.Addr i for any i, and Scaled an index with its scale. Imm is a literal that fits a sign-extended i32 immediate, Sh a literal shift count, which x86 masks. RegImm and RegSh are a Reg, or an Imm and a Sh for a literal that is one.
