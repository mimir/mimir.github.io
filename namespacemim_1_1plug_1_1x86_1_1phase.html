A side condition of a rule beyond what its slots state.

Enumerator
None 
Scale 

The elements the root mem.lea indexes have a size of 1, 2, 4, or 8.

Fold 

The root has a memory operand form and sits at its inner load, so its holes must be available there.

Rmw 

The inner load reads the root store's address on the memory the store takes, which nothing else uses.

Same 

The root has the register size of its operand.

Narrow 

The root's register is at most as wide as its operand's, but its size differs.

Widen 

The root's register is wider than its operand's.
