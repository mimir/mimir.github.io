Detailed Description

The machine facts of a native backend that the phases of mach need.

A value's class is Flags for mach.Flags, Stack for mach.Spill T, Fpr for math.F pe, and Gpr otherwise. A backend derives from it and adds its instructions, register file, ABI, and constraints.
