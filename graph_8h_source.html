66/// A PBQP problem graph.
67///
68/// Every node carries a cost vector with one entry per option. Every edge
69/// carries a cost matrix indexed by the options of its endpoints:
70///
71/// costs[src_option * num_options(dst) + dst_option]
72///
73/// The total cost of an assignment `x` is
74///
75/// sum_n c_n(x_n) + sum_{mn} M_mn(x_m, x_n)

78/// first. A Graph only grows; ids are handed out consecutively from 0.
