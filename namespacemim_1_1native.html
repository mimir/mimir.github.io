◆ Want

enum class mim::native::rules::Want : u8
strong

What a rule wants of the term a meta variable matches; a Param is bound but no operand.

A Lit is a Param that must be a literal. Reg is a general-purpose register, FReg a floating-point one, and Base/Index an address without/with an index. Addr is either address form, and Scaled an index with its scale. Imm is a literal that fits the backend's immediates, Sh a literal shift count, which the machine masks. RegImm and RegSh are a Reg, or an Imm and a Sh for a literal that is one.
